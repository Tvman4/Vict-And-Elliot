FAQ

What is Vict & Elliot?

Vict & Elliot is a Rick and Morty VR game starring Vict and Elliot. It uses Gorilla Tag locomotion, has Story Mode and Free Play Mode, and is built as a single-player experience.

When does the game come out?

The game is scheduled for November–December 2026 (Nov–Dec 26). A more exact date will be announced closer to launch.

What modes are in the game?

Two modes ship at launch: Story Mode (the campaign) and Free Play Mode (open sandbox play).

Do I need internet? Is there multiplayer?

No. There is no multiplayer, so the game does not need internet. You can play fully offline.

How does movement work?

The game uses Gorilla Tag locomotion — swing your arms to move through the world in VR.

What platform is it for?

Vict & Elliot is a VR game designed around standalone headset play (Quest-style, no PC required). Final store / headset list will be confirmed at launch.

Do I need a PC?

No PC is required to play. It’s built as a VR title you can run on a standalone headset.
